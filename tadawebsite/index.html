Where text-as-data meets the social sciences

A free online speaker series for researchers who apply NLP, machine learning and computational text analysis to political science, sociology, communication and neighbouring fields. Every second Wednesday at 17:00 Berlin time: a 20–30-minute talk, then discussion.
